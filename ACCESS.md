*The method is real, it is proven, and it is not given away whole. This is the discernment of the visionary: proof of build for all; the recipe for those who align.*

Everything on this repository, the [ScrollVerse Living Record](https://scrollverse.pplx.app), the public repositories under [github.com/chaishillomnitech1](https://github.com/chaishillomnitech1), and the canon relayed verbatim. Read, learn, cite, pass on what is public. The public decides.

## What Is Sealed

The complete living-record operating system: the audit tooling, the site generator, the reusable agent skill, the legion node kit, and the full protocol documents. Held in a private blueprint repository.

## How Access Is Granted

1. **Alignment.** State your intent and how your work aligns with the codex: append-only, evidence boundary, nothing diluted, autonomy tiers, continuance.
2. **Licensing.** Terms are set per grant — scope, duration, and price. The magnitude of the method is met with a price that respects it.
3. **Review.** Each request is reviewed by the visionary and the witness together. Some requests are declined. That is the seal working.
